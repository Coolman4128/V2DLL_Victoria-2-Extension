## Outcome

Build the shared job lifecycle and safe persistence mechanism chosen in S16.

## Inputs

Require S15/S16 contracts; use requirements cancellation, manual precedence and recovery rules.

Relative research paths above are under planning/. Read [requirements](../requirements.md), [architecture](../architecture.md), [acceptance](../acceptance.md) and root [agent rules](../../AGENTS.md). Research RVAs are snapshot leads, not permission to call an unproved ABI.

## Parallel lanes

1. Scheduler owner: implement game-time states, bounded work, retry reasons, idempotent receipts and parent/child jobs; no per-frame world scans.
2. Storage owner, in parallel: implement versioned templates/job schema, atomic writes, safe parsing, campaign/save binding and explicit import/recovery UI data.

## Integration and additional lane

Integrator: connect save/load callbacks, invalidate native references, reconcile queued/completed orders and test mid-command saves, older branch, corruption and missing metadata.

Use at most three workers plus integrator. One owner per file; only integrator edits shared source/projects/settings. Budget 15 minutes shared setup, up to 75 minutes overlapping lane work, and 30 minutes integration/validation/handoff. Third lanes described above run concurrently. Live game/debugger access and shared-file integration are serialized. Dependent work waits for its actual contract even within a lane.

## Exit checks

No double spending/units/orders on load; native troops always load; stale jobs cannot attach to wrong campaign or recycled IDs; recovery is explicit.
