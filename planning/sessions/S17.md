# S17 — Job scheduler and persistence implementation

- Status: NOT STARTED
- Type: required
- Estimated elapsed time: 120 minutes, including integration/validation (never over 2 hours).
- Prerequisite: S16 exit complete (required; cannot be skipped); also require the proved contracts below.

## Outcome

Build the shared job lifecycle and safe persistence mechanism chosen in S16.

## Inputs

Require S15/S16 contracts; use requirements cancellation, manual precedence and recovery rules.

Relative research paths above are under planning/. Read [requirements](../requirements.md), [architecture](../architecture.md), [acceptance](../acceptance.md) and root [agent rules](../../AGENTS.md). Research RVAs are snapshot leads, not permission to call an unproved ABI.

## Parallel lanes

1. Scheduler owner: implement game-time states, bounded work, retry reasons, idempotent receipts and parent/child jobs; no per-frame world scans.
2. Storage owner, in parallel: implement versioned templates/job schema, atomic writes, safe parsing, campaign/save binding and explicit import/recovery UI data.

## Integration and additional lane

Integrator: connect save/load callbacks, invalidate native references, reconcile queued/completed orders and test mid-command saves, older branch, corruption and missing metadata.

Use at most three workers plus integrator. One owner per file; only integrator edits shared source/projects/settings. Estimate 15 minutes for shared setup, up to 75 minutes for overlapping lane work, and 30 minutes for integration/validation/handoff. Third lanes described above run concurrently. Live game/debugger access and shared-file integration are serialized. Dependent work waits for its actual contract even within a lane.

## Exit checks

No double spending/units/orders on load; native troops always load; stale jobs cannot attach to wrong campaign or recycled IDs; recovery is explicit.

## Handoff and overrun

Record schema and migration rules, fault-injection tests and native save traces. If serialization/lifecycle work exceeds its estimate, checkpoint the incomplete result and continue with a smaller deliverable in the same family. Do not defer persistence until after features ship.

Update [status](../status.md) and write planning/handoffs/S17.md: actual elapsed time, changed paths, commit/worktree state, exact checks/results, artifact/save hashes, decisions, remaining uncertainty and next packet. A failed experiment may be complete; an unimplemented required feature may not. Use [execution control](../execution-control.md) to record the session estimate, elapsed time and retrospective. Estimates are advisory. At more than two hours, pause once for an independent timing review, then resume the same objective. At 2.5x estimate, reduce scope and continue with a smaller deliverable. Reviews calibrate timing only and do not add work. Sessions run serially; workers never start the next packet independently.
