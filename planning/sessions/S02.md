## Outcome

Prove which existing hooks work on the local executable and create a reliable boundary for new patches.

## Inputs

Use environment.md, patch-policy.md, current installers, minhook sources and the S01 manifest. Four matching bytes samples are insufficient.

Relative research paths above are under planning/. Read [requirements](../requirements.md), [architecture](../architecture.md), [acceptance](../acceptance.md) and root [agent rules](../../AGENTS.md). Research RVAs are snapshot leads, not permission to call an unproved ABI.

## Parallel lanes

1. Binary owner: generate a PE-aware read-only signature report for every retained/required instruction hook, byte patch, vtable slot and resume boundary; identify differences from upstream's reference binary and any prepatched gameplay behavior.
2. Boundary owner, in parallel: design AddressProfile/new-feature dependency validation and tests for mismatch, partial-install rollback and register/stack contracts. Review nullable expect and relative-instruction trampoline issues without rewriting frozen hooks.

## Integration and additional lane

Integrator: implement only narrow required validation/contract fixes and new-feature install seam; keep frozen correction semantics. Validate the complete active profile before a staged current-source launch. Do not mask mismatches by copying arbitrary bytes into expected signatures.

Use at most three workers plus integrator. One owner per file; only integrator edits shared source/projects/settings. Budget 15 minutes shared setup, up to 75 minutes overlapping lane work, and 30 minutes integration/validation/handoff. Third lanes described above run concurrently. Live game/debugger access and shared-file integration are serialized. Dependent work waits for its actual contract even within a lane.

## Exit checks

Known retained patch sites supported or explicitly blocked; startup reports applied/skipped sites accurately; synthetic mismatch installs nothing for its dependent feature. Controlled baseline launch uses verified profile.
