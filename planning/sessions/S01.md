Use at most three workers plus integrator. One owner per file; only integrator edits shared source/projects/settings. Budget 15 minutes shared setup, up to 45 minutes overlapping lane work, and 30 minutes integration/validation/handoff. Third lanes described above run concurrently. Live game/debugger access and shared-file integration are serialized. Dependent work waits for its actual contract even within a lane.
